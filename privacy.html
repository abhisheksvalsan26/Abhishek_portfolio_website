Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This is a personal portfolio. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Fonts, icons, images and videos are all served from this site, so no other company sees your visit.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect its service. See Netlify's own privacy policy for details.

If you contact me

If you email, call or message me on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, I use your details only to reply to you. I don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone.

Questions or removal requests

Email abhisheksvalsan@gmail.com and I will answer or delete our correspondence on request.
