    How I work

    Good marketing is rarely one big idea. It's a loop of finding the gap, testing against it and keeping what the numbers back up.

    1. Audit

      Break down the funnel, the numbers and what competitors are doing better.

    2. Plan

      Turn the gaps into a plan: content pillars, channels, budgets and the KPIs that matter.

    3. Launch

      Run campaigns across Meta, Google and organic, with creative built to be tested.

    4. Optimise

      Read results every week, cut what doesn't work and move budget to what does.
