How I work

Good marketing is rarely one big idea. It's a loop of finding the gap, testing against it and keeping what the numbers support.

  1. 1

    Audit

    I start with the numbers: your funnel, your ad account, Search Console and what competitors do better.

  2. 2

    Plan and launch

    A plan with clear targets, then campaigns, pages and content built so they can be tested.

  3. 3

    Measure and improve

    I read the results every week, cut what doesn't work and move budget to what does.
